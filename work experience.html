University of Exeter Student Representative

As part of the university's open days, student representatives help guide prospecting students around the engineering facilities, explaining how their course might be relevant to their interests, along with answering questions of any sort the guests and their families might be curious of. Such tasks include:

  • Talking about student life
  • Talking about accommodation
  • Talking about the details of their course
  • Representing the university
  • Maintaining answers to a professional standard
  • Ensuring answers and concise and to the point, delivering the exact amount of information the asker wants
