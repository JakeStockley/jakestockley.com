Welcome to jakestockley.com!

Who are you and what do you do?

My name is Jake Stockley. I am a mechanical engineering student currently studying at the University of Exeter.

What is this website for?

I'm glad you asked! This website serves a number of purposes, listed below:

  • To showcase my achievements, qualifications and skills
  • Easy access to showcase my projects and creations
  • A library of my notes, formula sheets and other revision materials for fellow students
  • For people to get to know me better, or who have similar interests
  • To provide easy contact options, and a list of all my socials. If a social media account is not listed on my website, it's not me!

Did you make this website yourself?

Yes I did! This website is hand-coded. I decided not to use a website-creator for various reasons. More information on my website page.

How can I navigate this site?

Hovering or clicking over the topics on the top bar will show drop-down menus which can choose specific pages. Clicking the "J" logo in the top left will take you back to the homepage.

I've found a spelling mistake/bug!

As this website is self-made, it's likely to have mistakes. If you find one, please email me (preferred) or message me on a social of your choice. A full list of contact options can be found on the contacts page.
