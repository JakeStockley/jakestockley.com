I have always had a fascination with biology, be it bugs, trees, lichen or mammals. Biology was my favourite (and best) subject during A-Levels and I used to read through many encyclopedias when I was in primary school about biology. Now with my digital camera I can photograph and capture the creatures I am so fascinated by.

My personal favourite group of animals are the Cnidarians. This phylum includes:

  • Jellyfish
  • Corals
  • Sea Firs
  • Sea Anenomes
  • Hydras
Out of all of them, Sea Anenomes are my above all favorite animals, hence my many photographs below of them. The local costaline and beaches have many perfect and varied habbitats for many of them to thrive and flourish. An expansive list of most Cnidarians living in UK waters can be found on the MarLIN website.
