Speleology, more commonly know as caving, is an extremely enjoyable and rewarding way I spend my weekends. Currently a member of Exeter University's Speleological Society I go on trips all over the UK, and hope to continue my trips past graduation. Below is a collection of caves and some pictures. If anyone is considering taking caving up as a hobby, I would strongly encourage them to see if they have a local club nearby.
